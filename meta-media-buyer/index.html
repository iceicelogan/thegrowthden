What the numbers say

  • Meta is most of the budget for a DTC brand. Triple Whale's 2025 benchmarks, built on $18.4 billion in spend from 33,000+ brands, put Meta at 68.3% of ad spend, and Common Thread Collective's Q1 2026 benchmark of 299 brands found the median brand puts 72% of paid media on Meta. (Triple Whale, 2025 Ecommerce Benchmarks; Common Thread Collective, April 2026)
  • The platform is changing under the account. Meta's own Advantage+ automated solutions went from a $60 billion annual run-rate in October 2025 to over $75 billion by July 2026, and its CFO said most advertisers still use automation for only part of their campaigns. Account structure that was right two years ago usually isn't now. (Meta, Q3 2025 earnings call; Meta, Q2 2026 earnings call)
  • Automation isn't a set-and-forget answer either. Tinuiti's Q4 2025 benchmark found 27% of retail Meta spend went to Advantage+ sales campaigns, down from 38% in Q1 2025, as advertisers rebalanced. Agency client data. (Tinuiti, Q4 2025 Digital Ads Benchmark)
  • Platform numbers and real numbers diverge in both directions. Haus found Meta's 7-day-click reporting under-counted incremental revenue by about 15% across 640 tests; Stella found some brands with platform ROAS above 4.0 had incrementality below 1.0. That is why the reporting I build leads with MER. (Haus, July 2025; Stella, August 2025)
  • Brands are bringing media in-house. The ANA's 2023 in-house agency report found 54% of in-house agencies handle some media planning and buying, and 65% of brands had moved work from external agencies in-house. (ANA, May 2023)

An analyst on where the budget goes

For the vast majority of advertisers, the question is not whether they should spend money on Meta's apps—the question is how much they should spend.

Max Willens, Principal Analyst, EMARKETER, in EMARKETER's April 2026 forecast
