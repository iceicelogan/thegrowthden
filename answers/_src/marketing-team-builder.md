Most brands get advice from one place and execution from another, and something always gets lost between them. I do both. Clients usually hire me for one thing and keep me around for more, because the work gets done and they like working with me.

## How we start

1. Write down the work that needs doing every week, not the title you think you need. Most "head of marketing" job posts turn out to be a media buyer and a creative owner.
2. I map the roles against that list, in the order they pay for themselves.
3. Build the system first: the scorecard, the creative loop, the launch workflow, with whoever is on the team today.
4. Write the job description from the system. Interview rubric and onboarding plan come with it.
5. Hire into it. I sit in the interviews if you want, and the new person's first week is spent running something that already works.
