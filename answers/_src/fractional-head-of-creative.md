## Questions people ask

### Why does creative matter so much on Meta now?
On Meta today, the creative does most of the targeting. The algorithm reads your ad to decide who sees it, so ten ads that say the same thing reach the same people. Different angles open up different audiences.

### Will AI-made ads look like AI slop?
Not if the inputs are right. Slop comes from asking a model to make something out of nothing. I give it the brand, the customer, what's been tried and what the founder won't do before it writes a word.
