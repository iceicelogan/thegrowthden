A fractional head of growth strategy for a DTC or e-commerce brand

By Logan Ice · Last updated October 5, 2026

If you're a growth-stage DTC brand that needs senior strategy without an expensive full-time hire, a fractional growth advisor is usually the right call. I'm Logan Ice, and in this seat I decide with you where the next marketing dollar goes, then help make it happen. It starts at $7,500 a month.

What I do

  • A pre-audit read of every channel. I judge the business on MER (total revenue against total marketing cost) and contribution margin rather than living inside one platform's attribution.
  • A 90-day plan written as bets. Each one has a cost, a way to tell if it's working, and a date we stop if it isn't.
  • A "what to do" dashboard that surfaces actions and recent numbers first, with the charts tucked behind it.
  • A weekly leadership session with you and your team.

Why strategy and execution from one person

Most brands get advice from one place and execution from another, and something always gets lost between them. I do both. Clients usually hire me for one thing and keep me around for more, because the work gets done and they like working with me.

What the numbers say

  • Most small businesses are running marketing in the margins. In Constant Contact's 2024 survey of 1,300+ small businesses, 56% said they have an hour or less a day for marketing and 73% lacked confidence in their marketing strategy. (Constant Contact, April 2024)
  • Even senior marketers rate themselves weakest at the growth part. In The CMO Survey's 2025 report, "managing marketing as a growth engine" was the lowest-rated skill among senior marketing leaders, and 64% named proving marketing's financial impact as their top challenge. (The CMO Survey, 2025)
  • One growth owner beats a committee. McKinsey's 2025 C-suite survey found companies with a single growth-oriented role on the executive committee see up to 2.3 times more growth than those that split it across several. (McKinsey, June 2025)
  • The seat is empty more often than you'd think. Forrester's 2026 analysis found only 52% of Fortune 500 companies have a marketing executive on the executive team or reporting to the CEO, and 23% changed their senior marketing leader in the past year. (Forrester, July 2026)
  • Budgets aren't growing to meet the job. Among the 401 marketing leaders in Gartner's 2026 CMO Spend Survey, budgets sat at 7.8% of company revenue and 56% said that wasn't enough to deliver their strategy. Large-company sample. (Gartner, May 2026)

A Gartner analyst on what the tools don't do

AI can help marketers optimize faster, but optimization is not the same as strategy.

Ewan McIntyre, VP Analyst and Chief of Research, Gartner Marketing, in Gartner's June 2026 survey release

How we start

  1. Book a call and tell me the number you need moved and what you've already tried.
  2. Give me read access to the ad accounts, Shopify and whatever finance uses for margin. I'll have my read of the business inside two weeks.
  3. Agree the contribution margin number with finance, so break-even MER and the target are set before anything else.
  4. Get the first 90 days as a list of bets, each with a cost, a success measure and a stop date.
  5. Start the weekly session. By month three your team is reading the scorecard without me explaining it.
