The Machine — how Meta decides which ad you see

Every time you open Instagram or Facebook, millions of ads are technically eligible to fill the slot in front of you. In roughly 100 milliseconds, Meta's delivery system collapses those millions down to one: retrieval models pull a few thousand plausible candidates, ranking models score what you're actually likely to do with each, a value equation blends bid, predicted action rate and ad quality, and an auction clears — with the winner paying the minimum needed to win.

The Machine is an interactive walkthrough of that pipeline: watch the collapse happen, run the auction yourself against simulated competitors, see why creative has become the targeting, and scan any brand's public signals the way the delivery system might read them. Every claim is graded — Meta-published, independently measured, or practitioner folklore — so you always know which parts are documented and which are industry legend.
