About

Strategy and execution, from the same person.

I'm Logan Ice. I've spent my career in growth at P&G, Varsity Tutors (Series A through C, three cities to an international brand, later a $1.4B IPO), Slalom, Little Passports, Wuffes (where we went from $14M to $40M+) and Equifax. Now I run The Growth Den out of St. Louis.

What I kept noticing is that brands don't usually need another full-time leader. They need someone good who can plug in, figure out what's actually going on, and then go do something about it. Sometimes that's strategy, sometimes it's creative, and sometimes it's rolling up my sleeves in Ads Manager.

I build a lot with AI, and I teach teams to build with it too. But the part that matters most is still human: sitting in the meeting and noticing why the founder is really asking for "more ads."
