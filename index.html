Questions people ask

The short answers.

What does a fractional growth advisor do?

I plug into your business in whatever seat growth needs most. Sometimes that's head of strategy, deciding where the next dollar goes. Sometimes it's head of creative, building a pipeline that stops your ads looking like everyone else's. Sometimes you already have a growth lead and need someone great in Ads Manager. Either way you get the strategy and the execution from the same person.

How much does The Growth Den cost?

Engagements start at $7,500 a month, whichever seat I'm in. Where it lands above that depends on how many seats I'm covering and how much building is involved. Ad spend and software stay in your accounts and aren't part of the fee. Taking a whole new product to market is a bigger custom program, and we'd scope that together.

Do you just advise, or do you do the work?

Both, and that's the point. Most advisors hand you a plan and leave you to execute it. I'll write the plan and then go build the dashboard, launch the ads, set up the creative workflow and train whoever on your team will run it.

Can you work alongside our existing growth lead or agency?

Yes. A lot of my work is filling the gap next to someone who's already there, whether that's a stretched-thin growth lead who needs a strategist, or a team that has strategy covered and needs hands-on media buying and tooling.

Are you an agency?

No, and I don't particularly like the agency model. Agencies build everything inside their own accounts and their own heads, and the day you stop paying you find out how little of it belongs to you. I build the systems with your team, in your accounts, and teach your people to run them, so what we build is yours whether I'm around or not.

Who do you work with?

Founders and marketing leaders at growth-stage DTC and e-commerce brands. I've done growth at P&G, Varsity Tutors, Little Passports, Wuffes and Equifax, so I'm comfortable anywhere from a scrappy team of three to a big org.

How do you use AI in the work?

I build simple, cheap-to-run AI tools into how your team works: creative pipelines, reporting dashboards, launch workflows, incrementality checks. You own them and I teach your team to use them. The judgment about why a founder is really asking for something stays human.

How long do engagements last?

As long as it's useful. Clients usually hire me for one thing and keep me around for more, because the work gets done and they like working with me. There's no six-month exit plan built in.
