If your ads look like everyone else's and your team can't produce new angles fast enough, you need a fractional head of creative who can also run the production. I'm Logan Ice. In this seat I build a creative engine that finds genuinely different angles, tests them before they go live, and turns the winners into finished ads quickly with AI. It starts at $7,500 a month.

What I do

  • A creative audit of what's actually winning in your account and why.

What the numbers say

  • Most ads don't work, so volume and difference matter. Across 550,000+ ads and about $1.3 billion in Meta spend, Motion's 2026 benchmarks found only 5% to 8% of ads become real winners and about half never get meaningful spend. Vendor data. (Motion, 2026 Creative Benchmarks)
  • Creative is the biggest lever you control. NCSolutions' analysis of nearly 450 CPG campaigns found creative accounted for 49% of incremental sales, ahead of brand, reach and targeting; Kantar ranks creative quality the top element within the marketer's control. (NCSolutions, August 2023; Kantar, December 2024)
  • Teams are being asked for more creative with the same people. HubSpot's 2026 State of Marketing found 83.5% of marketers say they're expected to produce more content, and most companies won't add significant marketing headcount in 2026. (HubSpot, 2026)
  • AI production is now the norm on Meta. On its Q2 2026 earnings call Meta said 9 million small businesses use at least one of its AI ad creative tools, and advertisers using its video generation features grew 20% quarter over quarter in late 2025. (Meta, Q2 2026 earnings call transcript; Meta, Q3 2025 transcript)

How we start

  1. Give me read access to the ad account. I'll audit what's actually winning and why, not what the agency report says.
  2. Name the default angle together, the one everyone in your category runs. It gets written down.
  3. Run the first storm and persona round with your creative owner in the room, so they see the loop before they own it.
  4. Produce the first batch from the winners, in your accounts, with the Higgsfield-to-CapCut path or whatever fits your brand.
  5. Set the cadence. Monthly to start, then as often as the account can test.

Questions people ask

Why does creative matter so much on Meta now?

On Meta today, the creative does most of the targeting. The algorithm reads your ad to decide who sees it, so ten ads that say the same thing reach the same people. Different angles open up different audiences.

Will AI-made ads look like AI slop?

Not if the inputs are right. Slop comes from asking a model to make something out of nothing. I give it the brand, the customer, what's been tried and what the founder won't do before it writes a word.
