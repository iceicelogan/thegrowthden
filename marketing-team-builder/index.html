What the numbers say

  • Hiring is the hard part. In The CMO Survey's 2025 report, hiring top talent was the biggest people challenge for marketing leaders at 41.2%, and the share of planned hires that are full-time fell as contractor hiring rose. (The CMO Survey, 2025)
  • Senior roles take longer. Across 54 million applications and 93,000 jobs, Ashby's 2026 benchmarks found senior roles take 37% longer to fill than junior ones. Tech-heavy sample. (Ashby, May 2026)
  • A wrong hire is expensive. Gallup puts the cost of replacing an employee at one-half to two times their annual salary; SHRM's benchmark puts the average cost per hire at about $4,700 before you count the ramp. (Gallup, 2019; SHRM, April 2022)
  • The process has to exist before the tools help. Gartner's 2026 survey of 401 marketing leaders found 70% say their internal processes aren't mature enough to implement and scale AI, and lack of internal AI expertise is the top barrier. Large-company sample. (Gartner, June 2026)
  • Training is the gap everywhere. The Marketing AI Institute's 2025 survey of 1,882 marketers found only 32% of organizations offer AI training for marketing, and lack of training was the top adoption barrier for the fifth year running. (Marketing AI Institute, 2025)

A Gartner analyst on tools versus teams

AI is not a shortcut around marketing capability. The organizations that will pull ahead are those that pair AI investment with the people, processes and discipline required to turn it into business results.

Ewan McIntyre, VP Analyst and Chief of Research, Gartner Marketing, in Gartner's June 2026 release

How we start

  1. Write down the work that needs doing every week, not the title you think you need. Most "head of marketing" job posts turn out to be a media buyer and a creative owner.
  2. I map the roles against that list, in the order they pay for themselves.
  3. Build the system first: the scorecard, the creative loop, the launch workflow, with whoever is on the team today.
  4. Write the job description from the system. Interview rubric and onboarding plan come with it.
  5. Hire into it. I sit in the interviews if you want, and the new person's first week is spent running something that already works.
